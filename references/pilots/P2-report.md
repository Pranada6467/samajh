# P2 report: call v2, a call real people can finish

2026-11-10, P2 builder. Edited `samajh/dialogue/`, `data/prompts/`, `tests/dialogue/`; wrote this file, `P2-lines-for-review.md` and §5 of `P2-contract.md`. No Gnani calls; OpenRouter about 0 USD (one `--llm` rerun of the judge's probe; the rules settled every turn). Everything here is synthetic text, not speech. No fluent human has read a line. Tamil and Kannada are not human-reviewed.

## 1. What changed

- **Who is believed** (`interpret.py`). At the first-name question a reply is a yes only if it claims to be the borrower ("हाँ जी", "बोल रहा हूँ", "मैं ही हूँ") and holds nothing else except a short list of courtesies, or one or two words beside "I" (the name). Relation words, third-person words and possessives ("रमेश की वाइफ़", "Ramesh's brother", "उनका") end the call with `end.third_party`, which gives only the first name and "customer care". The re-ask now asks "are you Ramesh yourself?", not "is he on the line?". The birth-year step checks relation and third-person words before the year. The LLM is never asked at this step.
- **Refusals** (`interpret.py`, `_lexicon.py`). A refusal, a request for a person, or a request for a later call ends the call. Without a stated time the bot asks "when should we call?" once; the reply is kept as `callback_time`. "I am driving" ends the call as `call_back_later`. A third-person or habitual narration ("वो लोग हमेशा बोलते हैं", "they always hang up") makes refusal words an answer. A first-person past ("मैंने कहा था") gets one "क्या हम बात जारी रखें?". "Who is this?" and "is this a fraud call?" get `verify.*`, then the question again (twice at most). "Be quick" continues the call. A language request ends the call as `language_switch` with the language recorded. Two silences end it as `no_response`. The LLM fallback now has a 2.5 s wall-clock limit. On a failure or a timeout the turn keeps the word lists' reading and goes into `degraded_turns`. The LLM can also clear a weak stop signal.
- **Opening.** Four short lines after the yes: who, not sales/feedback, recorded with the written-notice pointer, "क्या दो-तीन मिनट बात करें?". That is 48 words before the customer's second turn, **19.2 s at an assumed 2.5 words/s** (test-pinned for Hindi and Hinglish). Moved lines: "never OTP/PIN" sits with the birth-year question, where a cautious customer needs it. "Stop or ask for a person at any time" comes with the first mention of the loan, before any question about it. "How to check the call" is said on request (`verify.*`) and at every completed close (`close.verify`). This is still honest under DESIGN §5 "What grounds the opening": the AI line, not-sales, purpose, recording and notice pointer all come before the go-ahead, so they precede the consent DPDP s.5-6 asks about. The right to decline is the go-ahead question itself. The only datum taken before "stop any time" is said is the birth year, which the customer may refuse. **An informed one-product call is 147.6 s** by word count (bot plus customer, `call_seconds`; script in `tests/dialogue/fixtures/p2_calls/informed_hindi.txt`). That is under 150 s by 2.4 s. `call_seconds` uses `Turn.start_s/end_s` where the phone fills them.
- **Teach-back.** "Took nothing", "don't know" or an unclear answer at recall gets one follow-up (`open_recall.nothing`), then consent, then the close; no product question is asked. "कौन सी चीज़? मैंने तो कुछ नहीं लिया" at any product question jumps to consent. The riddle is gone ("वो क्या है? अपने शब्दों में बताइए।"). The recall question asks whether anything was taken, where it used to presuppose "और क्या-क्या". The provider-choice question is skipped when the customer says it was their own choice. A payment-frequency question is new (`asks=payment_frequency`). A bare yes or no to either either/or question is asked again. "पता नहीं" is never asked again (test-pinned over a whole call).
- **Amounts.** The read-back follows the premium answer at once and says what the figure is for ("तो आपने उसके लिए बावन हज़ार रुपये दिए, यानी 52,000। सही है?"). If the two readings differ, or only one holds a figure, the bot says `premium.say_again` once per product, then reads back. One yes and one no at a read-back repeat it. A reply that only repeats the figure inside other words ("कौन सा बावन हज़ार?") is not a confirmation, and yes plus another figure no longer replaces the premium. "पचास बावन हज़ार के आसपास" is read back as said (`read_back.confirm_about`, which round-trips through P3a's `parse_approx_amount`, test-pinned). **Coordinator additions:** a stated cover figure is read back and confirmed (`read_back.cover_confirm`, `step=read_back`, tagged through `cover_read_back_question()`: `Question.cover` until P3a names a value; **pending**). The say-again is tagged `asks=premium` like the first asking.

- No independent review of this package yet. The spec's reviewer subagent was not run, by instruction.
