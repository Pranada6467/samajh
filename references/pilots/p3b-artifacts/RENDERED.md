## What was run

1. The steps of `uv run python -m samajh.ops demo`, one at a time, into a fresh state: `init` (the loan book in `data/loanbook`, seed 85, batch `B-2027H1-ec2bdb3e`) and the demo's nine weekly `draw`s (11 January to 8 March 2027, default rate). The draws are byte-identical to those `ops demo` writes, and `ops verify` says every draw matches the book.
2. Phone sessions copied into `STATE/sessions` (case file, session log and recording only; the originals were not touched): the 44 folders of this machine (`.samajh/sessions`) and one loan-book call, session `20261113T040121-6089ad69eab2`, the call worker's replay on loan SYN-PL-270107-0008 after the fix round (from that worker's scratch state `B`; its earlier replay on the same loan, `20261113T032807-e802beadf2bc`, was left out: the loan's chain ends at the first completed call). Then `ops ingest`, **before** the simulated calls: 1 session on a drawn loan, 10 outside the sample, 34 skipped (no case file). The loan-book call is attempt 1 on SYN-PL-270107-0008 (drawn in D-2027-01-11, call date 11 January 2027, day 4 from the sale).
3. `ops run` (scripted customers, scripted extraction, no model), then `ops ingest` again (no change) and `ops verify`. The attempt log equals the one `ops demo` writes on every one of the other 79 sampled loans (112 attempts, identical). On SYN-PL-270107-0008 the demo's simulator would have made one attempt that ended `wrong_party`; here the phone call took that place, so the loan has one attempt, completed and referred. Why this order: with `ops demo` run first, the simulator's `wrong_party` ends that loan's chain and ingest refuses the phone call ("the loan's attempts had already ended"); `LoanBook.callable()` on a finished demo state lists no loan at all, so no new loan-book call could be placed on it either.
4. Six reviewer decisions were recorded through the review API, with the same notes as in the earlier render (reviewer "Asha Reviewer (synthetic)"; every decision is a made-up example). The sixth is on the loan-book call `20261113T040121-6089ad69eab2`, which is now a counted referral.
5. A review-only server (the review API and `web/`, no phone routes, no Gnani key) on port 8131, then `node tests/ops/page_shots.mjs` twice: once for the first simulated case in the queue, `TEST-CALL-SYN-PL-270203-0101-tb` (`case-*`), once for the loan-book call (`phone-case-*`).
