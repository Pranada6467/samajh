What this report is and is not

Simulated batch: the loan book, the customers and every call are synthetic. No real customer was called.

  • The loan book, the customers and the calls are synthetic and simulated. No real lender, customer or call is behind any number here.
  • Customer replies come from scripted phrase banks. They are text only: no speech was recorded or transcribed.
  • The share of each finding follows from the mix of customers the simulation assumes. It says nothing about any real lender.
  • Each interval is a 95% Wilson interval for sampling alone. It does not cover errors in reading the call or customers who were not reached.
  • Findings describe customers who completed the call. Customers who did not answer, declined or ended the call early may differ.
  • A referral is a request for a person to look at one call, and nothing more.
