What this report is and is not

Synthetic batch: the loan book and the customers are synthetic. Calls marked Simulated were scripted; calls marked Phone call were made on the browser phone with synthetic records.

  • The loan book, the customers and the calls are synthetic and simulated. No real lender, customer or call is behind any number here.
  • Customer replies come from scripted phrase banks. They are text only: no speech was recorded or transcribed.
  • The share of each finding follows from the mix of customers the simulation assumes. It says nothing about any real lender.
  • Each interval is a 95% Wilson interval for sampling among eligible sales only (sales a draw covered). It does not cover sales no draw covered, errors in reading the call, or customers who were not reached.
  • Findings describe customers who completed the call. Customers who did not answer, declined or ended the call early may differ.
  • A referral is a request for a person to look at one call, and nothing more.
  • Reviewer decisions are counted, not estimated: they are one reviewer's view of the file, and no interval is given for them.
  • Calls marked Phone call were made on the browser phone against synthetic records, with recorded synthetic voices. They are not real customers either.

Synthetic date: the report is dated by the simulated book's clock, the day of the last call attempt.

Each draw took 40% of the sales made since the previous draw that were still within 30 days of the sale, so draw windows never overlap and every eligible sale had about the same chance of being sampled (between 37.5% and 41.7%, by rounding). Shares and intervals describe the 200 eligible sales only; the 0 sale(s) no draw covered are not represented. Shares are unweighted because the chances are equal up to rounding.

Findings: features and risks

para 85Y asks that the findings be used to review product features and risks. Each rule runs on every counted call. Undecided calls had a gap under the rule and no flag. Referred is the number of the flagged calls that went to a reviewer for that rule.

Risk coverage is thin. One question in the call bears directly on risk: whether the customer can get the money back (any time, after a period, or with a return), which shows what they believe happens to the money. Describing the product as a deposit also bears on it. The call asks nothing about exclusions, claim conditions or what happens to the cover if the loan is closed early.

Seriousness is the builders' ranking, not a regulatory scale: a belief about what the product is, whether it returns money, or how it was agreed to ranks above forgetting a figure.
