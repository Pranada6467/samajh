# Synthetic loan book

Generated by `uv run python -m samajh.ops book --seed 85`. Do not edit by hand: the same
seed gives the same bytes, and `BOOK.json` holds the sha256 of `book.jsonl`.

**Everything here is synthetic.** No lender, borrower, loan, branch or call is real. Every id
starts with `SYN-` and every name ends in "(synthetic)". There are no phone numbers, account
numbers, Aadhaar or PAN.

## Files

- `book.jsonl`: 200 sales of Namoona Bank (synthetic), one `BookLoan` per line, sold
  2027-01-04 to 2027-03-04. Each is a gold or personal loan with one
  credit-life product. This is all the sampler, the queue and the report read.
- `sim-customers.jsonl`: one `SimCustomer` per loan. This is the simulation's hidden truth: what
  the customer believes and how each call attempt ends. Only the call simulator reads it.
- `BOOK.json`: the book's metadata, the generator's parameters and the count of each archetype.

## What is assumed, not measured

**The mix of customers is the builders' assumption.** It estimates nothing about any real lender,
and no share in a report built on this book is evidence about real sales.

| Archetype | The customer | Base share | Problem | Loans |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `informed_full` | states everything on the record | 37% | no | 64 |
| `informed_ordinary` | knows the product, its cost and how they agreed; forgot cover, term or the window | 37% | no | 64 |
| `forgot_premium` | knows the product, forgot the premium | 5% | no | 10 |
| `not_named_unprompted` | does not name the product until asked about it | 5% | no | 10 |
| `cannot_say_how_agreed` | agrees they bought it, cannot say how they agreed | 4% | no | 6 |
| `told_it_is_a_deposit` | believes it is an FD or a security deposit | 2% | yes | 8 |
| `believes_investment` | believes it is an investment with a return | 1% | yes | 4 |
| `did_not_agree` | says they agreed only to the loan; half have no consent on the record | 2% | yes | 7 |
| `deduction_not_known` | premium deducted from the loan; thinks they paid separately or does not know | 2% | yes | 8 |
| `required_no_choice` | required for the loan, and not told of a choice of provider | 1% | yes | 4 |
| `expects_money_back` | wrongly expects the money back | 1% | yes | 4 |
| `wrong_cancel_window` | states a different cancellation window | 1% | yes | 2 |
| `coached` | says they were told what to answer | 1% | yes | 4 |
| `knows_almost_nothing` | answers "don't know" to almost everything | 1% | yes | 3 |
| `evasive` | gives no usable answer to anything | 1% | yes | 2 |

**One branch and one channel carry more problems, by assumption.** At branch `SYN-BR-04` and
in the `agent` channel each problem archetype is 4 times likelier (both
factors apply together). This exists so the report's breakdown has something to show.

**The reach probabilities are assumptions too.** Each attempt ends as below; an attempt that goes
unanswered, or where the customer asks for a later call, is followed by another, up to 3.
50% of the customers who decline do so after the identity checks.

| Outcome of one attempt | Chance |
|---|---|
| no_answer | 25% |
| call_back_later | 8% |
| wrong_party | 3% |
| not_confirmed | 3% |
| declined | 7% |
| incomplete | 5% |
| completed | 49% |

## Limits

- Customer replies come from the scenario phrase banks (`samajh/scenarios/phrases/`), so the text
  is templated: a few wordings per answer, Hindi and Hinglish only, model-written and
  model-reviewed with no human reviewer. Text only, no speech.
- Premiums, covers, terms, cancellation windows and years of birth are limited to the values the
  phrase banks can say. They are not a realistic distribution of loan sizes or prices.
- Every loan has exactly one product, and every product is credit-life cover.
