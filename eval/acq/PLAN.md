# ACQ plan: does the open call find what a yes/no call misses? (iteration 4, W3)

**Status:** frozen 2026-11-14 and run once (`eval/acq/runs/counted`; results in `eval/acq/REPORT.md`). Sections 1 to 10 are as frozen; section 11 records the review, plus one correction found after the run. Frozen in `data/acq/FROZEN.json` after the independent checker's plan review (section 9). One counted run. A rerun after any fix is labelled "post-fix rerun of an evaluation already seen".

## 1. The question and why P5 could not answer it
P5 found no difference between the open Samajh call and a closed yes/no call at level 0 (problem customers: both caught 25 of 26, p = 1.0; `eval/p5/REPORT.md` §3). The iteration 4 review (`references/reviews/iter4/technical-review.md` §3 item 6, §5) gives four reasons P5 could not have found one: its customers always contradicted a false statement (the writer saw the statement); its closed rule counted anything but a plain yes as caught; giving way was not matched between the arms; any `refer` counted as caught whatever its ground.

The product's theoretical edge is **recognition against recall**: a customer who cannot recall the premium or the term may still say yes when a figure is read to them, while an open question gives them nothing to say yes to. This experiment builds customers who do that, on text, and measures both arms on them.

## 2. Customers (`data/acq/`, built by `eval/acq/acq_build.py`, no LLM)
- **160 synthetic customers**, `ACQ-C001` to `ACQ-C160`, seed `samajh-acq-2026-11-14`. Half Hindi, half Hinglish within each kind. Kinds: `informed` 32, `knowledge_gap` 48, `misled` 40, `misled_with_gaps` 40.
- **Record:** one credit-life product per loan, obviously fake names and ids. The record values are the lender's.
- **Belief:** for each of 10 fields (kind, premium, payment source, frequency, required for the loan, cover, term, cancel window, money back, consent), one state: `correct` (matches the record), `false` (contradicts it) or `dk` (does not know).
  - informed: all correct. knowledge_gap: 1 to 3 fields dk (premium, cover, term, cancel window, money back, frequency). misled: 1 or 2 fields false (kind, premium, payment source, required, cover, money back). misled_with_gaps: 1 or 2 false and 1 or 2 dk.
  - Kind, payment source, required and consent are never dk (the product refers on not knowing them; this set keeps gaps on the fields where not knowing is an understanding gap). Consent is always correct.
- **Gold** (`gold.jsonl`): `problem` if any field is false; else `knowledge_gap_only` if any is dk; else `no_problem`. The **ground** is the false fields (problem) or the dk fields (knowledge gap only). A consistency check runs the rule engine (`samajh.rules.evaluate`) on perfect beliefs built from each belief and requires, for all 160, the gold tier (refer / understanding_gap / clear) and a flag naming every ground field. Gold is therefore consistent with the product's own tier table on perfect input; what the run measures is how far the call and extraction get from the customer's words to that result.

## 3. Two parameters, one seeded draw per customer and field
- **Recognition bias r** = P(says yes to a statement about a field they do not know). **Acquiescence a** = P(says yes to a statement that contradicts what they believe). Grid r, a ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1}; **every customer is played at every one of the 25 cells** (a full factorial, so each cell has all 160 customers and the pairs are within customer).
- Draws: `u_a(f)` and `u_r(f)` per customer and field, from sha256 of `seed|customer|field|axis`. The same draws drive both arms and every cell.

## 4. The knowledge writer (`eval/acq/acq_write.py`)
- One request per customer. It carries the customer's fact sheet (generated by template from the belief alone) and the open-call topics (paraphrases of the bot's questions, `data/acq/topics.json`). It never carries the record, a record value the customer does not hold, or any closed statement. The writer writes, per topic, `own` (what they say from what they know, or their own "don't know"), `again` (the same content in other words, for a re-ask) and `defer` (leaving it to the lender, with no fact).
- Two writer models from different labs, as in P5: `google/gemini-3.7-flash` and `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, each writing half the customers, balanced within kind and language. Results are also reported by writer.
- Mechanical checks only (empty, digits, length, script); up to two more attempts on a failure. No content is filtered or edited.

## 5. The deterministic player and the two arms
**Closed arm (symbolic).** Ten statements, one per field, each the record's value followed by "क्या यह सही है?" (`data/acq/closed_statements.jsonl`, for display). Each answer: correct → yes; false → yes if `u_a(f) < a`, else no; dk → yes if `u_r(f) < r`, else don't know. The reading is perfect: no text, no interpreter. This favours the closed arm (P5's misreadings of agreement with a negative statement cannot occur).
- **Strict rule (primary, fixed now):** `confirmed` only when all ten are yes; a no or a don't know is caught.
- **Lenient rule (reported beside it):** `confirmed` unless some answer is no; a don't know is not caught.

**Open arm (the Samajh call as built, on text).**
- *Entry, identical for every call:* `samajh.dialogue.start` with the default identity setting (no second check) and the word-list interpreter only; replies "हाँ जी।" to `right_party.ask`, "हाँ जी, मैं ख़ुद ही बात कर रहा/रही हूँ।" to `right_party.reask`, "हाँ जी, बोलिए।" to `disclosure.go_ahead`. The customer never says a name, so name matching cannot decide entry. Scoring starts at the first `open_recall.ask`. A call that does not reach it is `entry_failed`, counted and excluded.
- *After entry:* the interpreter the server uses (LLM turn classifier `mistralai/mistral-small-3.2-24b-instruct` over the word lists, no wall-clock limit), no second reading, then the real extractor (`openai/gpt-6-luna`) and rules (`samajh.pipeline.run_text`).
- *Replies:* the topic of the bot's last line comes from P5's frozen line mapping (`eval/p5/player.topic_for`). On a field topic the customer gives the writer's `defer` reply if `u_a(f) < a`, else `again` on a re-ask, else `own`. Non-field turns (read-back confirmations, "anything else", "shall we carry on", a mid-call who-check) get fixed short replies; an unmapped line gets "जी, समझ नहीं आया।" and is logged.
- **Giving way is matched with the same draw:** the customer who says yes to a contradicting statement on field f in the closed arm (`u_a(f) < a`) is the customer who defers on field f in the open arm. In the open arm the customer cannot know whether a belief matches the record (nothing is stated), so deference applies to every field with `u_a(f) < a`, correct ones included; in the closed arm a yes to a matching statement costs nothing. This asymmetry is a cost to the open arm and is reported (informed customers).
- **dk fields are not matched by one draw.** In the open arm a dk field is deferred when `u_a(f) < a`; in the closed arm its answer follows `u_r(f)`. The two arms are paired on dk fields only through the cell.
- **The open recall question asks "what, and for how much?".** When the kind is not deferred but `u_a(premium) < a`, the customer answers open recall with their `what_is_it` reply (no figure), so the premium is not revealed under a premium deferral. If the bot asks "anything else?" after open recall, the customer answers with the same recall reply as a re-ask, never with a canned "nothing".
- **r never enters the open arm.** No bot line states a record value under test (the product's rule), so there is nothing to recognise. Open calls therefore run per (customer, a): 800 calls; their results serve every r at that a.

## 6. What is true by construction, stated before the run
1. **r acts on the closed arm only.** The recognition premise is assumed, not tested. On knowledge-gap customers the closed arm confirms exactly those whose every dk field drew `u_r < r`; that count is arithmetic on the draws. What is measured is the open arm: whether the call, as built, turns the writers' own "don't know" into an `understanding_gap` naming the right field, and at what cost.
2. **Under a, the arms give way on the same fields.** A problem customer is confirmed by the closed arm only when every false field drew `u_a < a`; in exactly those draws the open customer defers on every false field. So on false beliefs the open arm is not expected to find what the closed arm confirms (open-only pairs b ≈ 0), except where a deferred field is revealed through another field's answer. The a-axis can only show a difference in **visibility**: whether the open call ends `incomplete` (sent to a human, "did not yield enough") where the closed call says `confirmed`.
3. The closed arm's 0 false referrals of informed customers is by construction (perfect reading, every answer yes). The closed arm also never ends early, always asks all ten statements, never defers on a correct or dk field, and counts "don't know" as caught.
4. Gold agrees with the rule engine on perfect input by construction (section 2).
5. **The r axis is arithmetic.** At a fixed a every r uses the same open calls; only the closed thresholds move. The 25 cells are 5 open measurements, each set against 5 closed thresholds. So "the gap grows with r" cannot fail at a fixed a and is not a test.
6. **At a > 0 almost every open call has a deferral** (knowledge-gap customers with no deferral: 4 of 48 at a = 0.25, 0 of 48 at a ≥ 0.5; checker's arithmetic), and a deferral ends a call non-clear (`incomplete`, or `declined` when the classifier hears "you'd know better" as a refusal). So at a > 0 the open arm's knowledge-gap finds (which need tier `understanding_gap`) collapse, and its "not clear" rate on problem customers equals the closed arm's confirmations by construction. Neither is a measurement of the call; both are reported as what deference costs each arm.
7. The open arm's false values are far from the record (premium at most half or at least double; cover at most 0.4 of the record) and stated plainly. That favours the open arm on false beliefs.

## 7. Scoring (`eval/acq/acq_score.py`, from the raw call files only)
Unit = customer. Per cell (r, a) and per kind, n and Wilson 95% intervals; no pooling across cells; entry failures and no-result calls reported and excluded from n. A cell whose exclusions pass 5% of its calls carries a banner. The scorer refuses an incomplete run.
- **Misses** (problem customers): open = not `refer`; closed = `confirmed`. Open misses are split into `incomplete`, `understanding_gap`, `clear` and ended early, by outcome (`declined` apart). A **silent miss** is `clear` or `understanding_gap`.
- **Knowledge gaps found** (knowledge_gap customers): open = tier `understanding_gap` with an understanding-gap flag naming a dk field; also `gap_flag_any_tier` (that flag whatever the tier). Closed = a dk field answered "don't know" (strict). Also open false referrals (`refer`), `incomplete`, `clear`.
- **False referrals of customers without a problem**: open `refer`; closed `caught` (strict and lenient). Open "not clear" on informed customers is printed as a cost line, never as a McNemar win.
- **Ground match and provenance:** an open referral is ground-matched only when a refer-tier flag's field (`FLAG_FIELD`, `eval/acq/acq_common.py`) is a gold ground field, its quote comes from the customer's own or again reply (not a defer reply, not a canned line), and, on a false field, its reason is not a `*_dont_know`. A closed catch is ground-matched when a caught field is a ground field. The any-source counts are printed beside the strict ones.
- Referral basis (customer statement or record alone), degraded turns, cache hits and `incomplete` gaps on a gold false field (`incomplete_on_ground`, descriptive visibility) are counted.
- No open-minus-closed rate difference is printed; only counts, per-100 figures with intervals, and McNemar lines (b = open only, c = closed only).

## 8. The prediction, written before the run, and what refutes it
**Prediction (from the task):** the open arm finds knowledge gaps and false beliefs that the closed arm confirms whenever r > 0 or a > 0, and the gap grows with them.

After the plan review (section 11), only part of it is testable with this harness. Each part:
- **H1 (knowledge gaps, primary).** At a = 0 and r ∈ {0.5, 0.75, 1}, on knowledge_gap customers: open gap_found and closed strict gap_found, paired, give b > c with exact McNemar p < 0.05 (P1). **Refuted** if any of those three cells has b ≤ c or p ≥ 0.05. The closed side is arithmetic on the draws (section 6 items 1, 5); the open side is the measurement: whether the call turns the customers' own "don't know" into an understanding gap on the right field. At r = 0.25 and at a > 0 the P1 lines are descriptive.
- **H1-cost.** At a = 0: open referrals and `incomplete` endings of informed and knowledge_gap customers, and silent misses of problem customers. No threshold; reported whatever the result.
- **H2 (false beliefs under acquiescence).** Decided before the run: under the matched draw a problem customer the closed arm confirms has deferred on every false field in the open arm, so open-only ground-matched referrals (b) can come only from leaks (e.g. a false belief said in another field's answer) and closed-only ones (c) from open misses. The prediction "the open arm finds false beliefs the closed arm confirms when a > 0" is therefore **expected to fail by construction**, and the run only counts the leaks. The sentence that retires it: "Under this harness's matched-deference model, by construction, the open call finds no false belief that the yes/no call confirms; when a customer gives way to the lender as readily in an open call as in a yes/no call, the open question cannot recover what they will not say." P2a at a = 0 (every r) is primary only as a miss count for the open arm (the closed arm catches every false field at a = 0 by construction).
- **H3 (visibility).** Decided before the run (section 6 item 6): at a ≥ 0.5 every open call has a deferral and ends non-clear, so "open does not pass what closed confirms" holds by construction for problem customers and is equally true of informed ones. Reported descriptively, always beside the informed not-clear rate at the same a; no p-value from it is a test.
- **Multiple testing.** The primary tests are the three H1 cells. The scorer prints several hundred other p-values under "descriptive, not a test"; none is quoted alone.

## 9. Order of work
1. Build the customers (`acq_build.py build`, `check`); consistency check 160 of 160.
2. Writer run (`acq_write.py run`), both models. Done 2026-11-14: 160 of 160, USD 0.79, one mechanical check left failing (C026 `no_devanagari`).
3. Player, runner and scorer with tests; a dry run on 4 customers, not a result (`eval/acq/runs/dryrun-unfrozen/`).
4. This plan reviewed by the independent checker; fixes applied (section 11).
5. Freeze: `data/acq/FROZEN.json` with the sha256 of every data file, every harness file (including the imported `eval/p5` modules) and the source tree the run uses. `acq_run.py` refuses on any mismatch.
6. One counted run, from a clean snapshot of a committed revision (`git archive` of `samajh/`, `data/prompts/`, `eval/p5/`, `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock` into a scratch directory, with `eval/acq`, `data/acq` copied in; the shared LLM cache and ledger via `SAMAJH_STATE_DIR`). Uncommitted edits in the shared worktree cannot reach it. A preflight runs the entry for all 160 customers and refuses unless all 160 reach open recall.
7. Score; the independent checker recomputes every count, interval and p-value from the raw call files with its own script.

## 11. Plan review and fixes
The independent checker (Opus, high effort, wrote none of the code) reviewed this plan, the data and the harness before the freeze (18 findings). What was done:
- **H1 refuted by construction at a > 0** (dk fields deferred under `u_a`, deferral ends non-understanding-gap): H1 is tested at a = 0 only; a > 0 is a cost (section 8); dk-field pairing stated (section 5).
- **"b does not fall as r rises" can never trigger:** dropped; r axis declared arithmetic (section 6 item 5).
- **H3 decided before the run:** demoted to descriptive, printed beside the informed rate (section 8).
- **Premium revealed under the kind draw** (131 of 138 recall replies state the figure): the recall reply follows the premium draw too (section 5).
- **Canned "nothing else" contradicts every customer:** replaced by the customer's recall reply (section 5).
- **Flags from a deferral could count as findings:** provenance rule (section 7).
- **Fact sheets of fee and deposit believers left out provider choice** (19 customers, a flag gold did not predict): fact sheets fixed and those customers rewritten.
- **Deferral often ends the call `declined`:** counted apart (section 7).
- **P3 on informed customers read as an open win:** dropped as a test; printed as a cost.
- **H2's retirement sentence:** reworded as "by construction"; P2 definitions aligned with the scorer.
- **Code not pinned:** frozen hashes of every harness file and the source tree, a refusing check, and a run from a committed snapshot (section 9 item 6).
- **Name matching still runs on entry replies:** a preflight over all 160 customers with the pinned code.
- **Said in the report, not changed:** both arms' built-in advantages (section 6 items 3, 7); the knowledge audit (0 dk replies state a figure; C100 and C146 replies blur their gap; the defer count is corrected below); multiple testing (section 8); implausible mixes such as a fee believer who knows the death cover.

**Correction after the counted run (checker, part 2):** the part-1 audit's "about 22 of 1,920 defer replies state a fact, each consistent with the belief" was wrong. Its pattern missed "साइन"; 58 consent `defer` replies name a method and 2 contradict their belief (C079, C100). Nothing in the frozen inputs was changed for it; the effect is reported in `REPORT.md` §3 and §4.

## 12. What may never be claimed, whatever the result
"Real customers"; a prevalence or field rate; "teach-back beats yes/no" in general; "mis-selling found"; any result on speech; any Tamil or Kannada number; that recognition bias exists (it is assumed).
